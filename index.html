Why Creative Acoustics

Why I Build Guitars

In this shop, wood and music speak the same language. Every instrument begins as living material — spruce, maple, walnut — and ends as a voice.

I started building guitars at the age of 14 with my Dad. We built an SG copy since that was my all-time favourite guitar at the time. It was a learning experience for both of us, using a book that my Mom found at a local bookstore. In the end, it turned out pretty well, though I've made a few tweaks to it since then, as my experience and knowledge increased.

As a musician and woodworker, there is no greater feeling than creating a beautiful instrument, putting the finishing touches on it, then plugging it in and playing it for the first time.

And when I receive feedback from happy clients, it's even better.

From the Falmouth workshop, James Omand has become the builder players across the Annapolis Valley come to — for one-of-a-kind guitars and basses, for hand-turned fittings that finish an instrument properly, and for honest guidance in choosing the wood behind the voice.

— James Omand, Creative Acoustics Ltd.

Services

Guitar Building

Custom guitars and basses built from the ground up: bodies, necks and headstocks shaped to how you play. Four, five and six-string basses, left-handed builds and one-off designs.

  • Custom bodies, necks & headstocks

Accessories

  • Control knobs & switch tips
  • Pickup rings & plates
  • Custom design details

Wood Selections

The wood sets the look and the voice of an instrument. Choose from figured tops, burls and exotic hardwoods, and get guidance on pairing them for the sound you're after.

  • Tonewood pairing guidance
